FAQ

Still stuck? Ping us on GitHub.

?Which version should I pick?

1.8.8 is the most stable and what most servers run. 1.8.8 WASM is the same game but faster on recent Chrome and Edge. 1.12.2 has newer blocks and mobs but is still early and buggy. 1.5.2 is the oldest and lightest.

?Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one and saves in your browser. An account only carries your settings between devices.

?Why no capes on 1.5.2?

1.5.2 uses an older profile format with no custom capes, so it gets the mod menu only.

?Is there 1.21?

Not yet. The 1.21 browser ports we tried freeze before the main menu. It'll be added once one actually works.

?Where are my worlds saved?

In your browser's storage for this site. Clearing site data deletes them, so back up anything you care about from inside the game.
